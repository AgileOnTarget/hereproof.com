What HereProof is

HereProof is a presence-backed selfie NFT app. It is built for moments that matter: meeting a friend, sharing an experience, showing up for someone, or preserving a memory that should carry more weight than an ordinary photo in your camera roll.

📸

Capture the moment

Take a selfie inside the app while you are together. The photo becomes the starting point for a HereProof, not just another image file.

🛡️

Verify the moment

The app is designed to verify that the moment was real. The goal is simple: move from “here is a picture” to “here is proof.”

🔒

Keep it forever

Once minted, the HereProof becomes a permanent digital object you can hold in your collection, share with others, or turn into a meaningful gift.
