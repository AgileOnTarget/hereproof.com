HereProof

Proof you were there.

HereProof is the simplest way to mark a moment so you can share it later. Open the app, take a photo, add an optional note or the city where you were, and send it on. The photo carries the context with it — no other app required on the other end.

What HereProof does

  • Capture a moment with the camera.
  • Add an optional note.
  • Optionally include the city you were in.
  • Share it through the iOS share sheet — Messages, Mail, Photos, wherever.

What HereProof doesn't do

  • No account. No sign-up. No password.
  • No server. HereProof never sends your data to us.
  • No tracking. No ads. No analytics.
  • Delete the app, delete your data.
