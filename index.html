What HereProof is

A small iPhone app for capturing and sharing a moment. Whether you're on your own, with someone, or in a whole group — HereProof turns what just happened into something you can send on with the context that makes it worth keeping.

📸

Capture the moment

Take a photo inside the app. It becomes the start of your HereProof, not just another image buried in your camera roll.

✍️

Add the context

Write a short note if you want. Toggle the city on or off. Your HereProof carries the when and where with it — all rendered onto the image itself.

📤

Share anywhere

Tap Share and pick whatever iOS app you already trust. The photo goes with the context baked in, so your friend doesn't need HereProof to see it.

How it works

HereProof is meant to feel simple on the surface and thoughtful underneath. Herph walks you through each step so the flow stays clear and friendly.

1

Start a HereProof

Open HereProof, tap Create a HereProof, grant the camera permission. Herph is right there.

2

Take the photo

Point, tap, review. Retake if you want. The photo stays on your device the whole time.

3

Add a note and (optionally) your city

A short caption and the city you're in. Both optional, both rendered into the image itself.

4

Share through iOS

The share sheet takes over. Messages, Mail, Photos, wherever — you decide. Herph celebrates the landing.
